# App catalog

All paths are under `apps/` on the [live site](https://alexarje.github.io/Oslo-Mobile-Orchestra/).

## Rhythm & timing

| App | Music | Technology |
|-----|--------|------------|
| [Conductor](https://alexarje.github.io/Oslo-Mobile-Orchestra/apps/conductor/) | Shared downbeat | 5 s countdown, musician URL with `start` + `bpm`, QR |
| [Your Part](https://alexarje.github.io/Oslo-Mobile-Orchestra/apps/part/) | Seating / roles | `?part=1`…`20` suggests an instrument |
| [Drumkit](https://alexarje.github.io/Oslo-Mobile-Orchestra/apps/drumkit/) | Hand drums | 8 synthesized pads |
| [Drum Sequencer Linear](https://alexarje.github.io/Oslo-Mobile-Orchestra/apps/drum-sequencer/) | Grid patterns | 4 tracks × 8 steps, swing, presets |
| [Drum Sequencer Circular](https://alexarje.github.io/Oslo-Mobile-Orchestra/apps/circular-drum/) | Ring patterns | 4 concentric rings × 16 steps |
| [Firefly](https://alexarje.github.io/Oslo-Mobile-Orchestra/apps/firefly/) | Synced clicks | Mic entrainment of rate & phase |
| [Delay Throw](https://alexarje.github.io/Oslo-Mobile-Orchestra/apps/delay-throw/) | Echo throws | Tap sample; X/Y = time & feedback; tilt |
