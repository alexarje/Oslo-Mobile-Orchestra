Oslo Mobile Orchestra

Web instruments for ~20 phones — synthesis, sensors & simple AI. Tap an app, then tap Start sound on each device.

  • Ensemble · Timing Conductor One person shares a link; everyone starts on the same beat.
  • Synthesis · Touch Synth Pad XY pad — waveform, filter & FM. Classic subtractive + modulation.
  • Synthesis · Accelerometer Motion Wah Tilt the phone to sweep a resonant filter (wah / vowel sounds).
  • Harmony · Gyroscope Drone Choir Pick a chord tone; rotate the phone to detune your drone in the ensemble.
  • AI · k-NN · Motion Train & Shake Record “still” vs “shake” — teach a classifier, then play by moving.
  • Synthesis · Granular · Tilt Granular Tilt Grain cloud texture — pitch and density from device orientation.
  • Rhythm · Touch Pulse Grid 16-step sequencer — each musician owns a different sound color.

Before the concert

Wi‑Fi: Same room network helps if you use Conductor links; other apps work offline.

iPhone: Allow motion/orientation when asked. Keep silent mode off for volume.

Android: Chrome works best. Disable battery saver for the browser tab during performance.

Volume: Set phones to ~70% so 20 devices balance in the room.
